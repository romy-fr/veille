Abstract

Disruptions to leading-strand synthesis impair and stall replication forks because DNA synthesis by the leading-strand polymerase, Pol ε, is essential for optimal template unwinding by the replicative CMG helicase1,2.

Main

DNA lesion bypass by leading-strand repriming was demonstrated for the core Escherichia coli replisome.

Reconstituting human replication fork restart

We reconstituted fork stalling.

PRIMPOL binds directly to CMG

The non-essentiality of the PRIMPOL RBMs for fork restart prompted us to explore an interaction with the core replisome.

Methods

Replication reactions were performed essentially as described previously.
